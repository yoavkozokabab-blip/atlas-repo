        Welcome to JARVIS

        JARVIS prepares your codebase for AI assistants — locally, with evidence-backed structure.

        1. What JARVIS does — Maps dependencies, architectural risk, and subsystems from your repo.
        2. Step 1: Choose repository — Paste your project folder path or try Demo Mode.
        3. Step 2: Scan — Builder Core indexes modules and import edges (runs on your machine).
        4. Step 3: Explore graph — 3D dependency view with risk coloring.
        5. Step 4: Ask Copilot — Grounded answers about risk, impact, and architecture.
        6. Step 5: Export prompt — Copy compact context for Claude, Codex, or Cursor.

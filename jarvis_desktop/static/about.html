Repository intelligence

About Atlas

Atlas is a local-first repository intelligence desktop product. It maps architecture, dependencies, risk and impact so developers and their AI coding tools begin with a grounded view of the codebase.

Why Atlas exists

Large repositories are expensive to understand repeatedly. Atlas prepares a compact, inspectable map before an AI assistant starts reading files and making claims.

Where the product is today

Atlas is in beta. The core desktop analysis is real; the team is improving trust, usability and validation with early users.

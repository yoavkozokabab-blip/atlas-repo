## Main Findings

- Build Plans improved only at the mostly-correct edge: 14/100 were MOSTLY_CORRECT, but none were strictly CORRECT.
- Investigation remains weak: 8/100 were MOSTLY_CORRECT, 26/100 were MISLEADING, and no sample was strictly CORRECT.
- Impact regressed under the expanded corpus: 0/100 were CORRECT, 20/100 were MISLEADING, and 11/100 were WRONG.
- High confidence is not a reliable correctness signal. High-confidence outputs had a 39.9 trust score and included 24 MISLEADING outputs.
- Unsupported language handling is clearer than before in that Kubernetes is labeled unsupported in graph health, but Impact still returned 11 WRONG target-resolution failures instead of an honest non-answer.
- Unknown mode is used frequently: 179/300 samples. It avoids some hard failures but often produces generic partial answers rather than actionable, grounded answers.

Q10: What parts of the codebase appear unrelated to Jarvis for Builders?
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Parts of the JARVIS codebase that appear unrelated to the Builders product vision:

1. Trading system (actions/trading_*.py, actions/phase45–55_actions.py)
   ~40% of the action surface. Covers live loops, backtests, execution
   investigation, patch workflows, and replay analysis. Entirely unrelated
   to project intelligence or builder tooling. Candidates for an optional
   plugin per the Phase 84 / Stage 0 plan.

2. Hebrew language classifier (brain/intent_classifier.py ~lines 1–500)
   ~150 Hebrew phrase rules built for a single user's personal setup.
   Not a product feature. Should become language-agnostic.

3. Voice-first infrastructure (voice/, ui/overlay_app.py, wakeword_loop.py)
   Significant engineering for push-to-talk, wakeword detection, and voice
   latency tracking. Deprioritised for a builder product (keyboard-first).

4. Computer control layer (computer_control_actions.py, phase63_desktop_actions.py)
   Clipboard, window focus/minimize. Not part of the intelligence layer.

5. Study/quiz system (actions/study_actions.py)
   Quiz mode, study notes, focus mode. Personal productivity, not builder tooling.

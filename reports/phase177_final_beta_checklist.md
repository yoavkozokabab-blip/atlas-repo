## Final Answer: Can Atlas Support Beta Users?

### 5 Supervised Users

**GO — after fixing B1–B8.**

Total engineering time: ~9 hours + infrastructure configuration. The trust integrity system is solid (10/10 attacks cleared). The intelligence works on real Python and TypeScript repos. The support contact exists. With supervision (onboarding calls, DM support, personal follow-up), the remaining UX friction is explainable.

**The one non-negotiable:** Fix B1 (demo default). It takes 5 minutes. Every beta user's first impression depends on it.

**What supervision covers:**
- Explaining internal vocabulary (Insertion confidence, Evidence score)
- Guiding users to the real-repo scan (away from the demo)
- Explaining what to do with the plan after copying
- Troubleshooting port conflicts and Windows issues

---

### 20 Supervised Users

**CONDITIONAL GO — after fixing B1–B21.**

At 20 users, supervision cannot compensate for product confusion. The product must make sense without explanation. The timeline for 20-user readiness is approximately 2–3 additional focused engineering days after the 5-user fixes.

**The critical path:**
1. Fix B1–B8 (9h) → 5 users
2. Fix B9–B21 (2–3 days) → 20 users

The intelligence is production-quality. The trust system is solid. The remaining work is entirely UX polish. None of it requires new features or intelligence changes.
